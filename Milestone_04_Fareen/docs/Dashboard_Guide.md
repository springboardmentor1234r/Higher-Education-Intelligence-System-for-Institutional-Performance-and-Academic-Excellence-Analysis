# EduVision DV – Dashboard Guide

## Overview

EduVision DV is a Tableau-based higher education analytics project designed to analyze university ranking, research, student, and country-level information.

The project contains four main dashboards.

---

## 1. University Overview Dashboard

### Purpose

Provides an overall view of university performance.

### Main KPIs

- Global Ranking Score
- Research Impact Score
- Academic Reputation Score
- Faculty-to-Student Ratio
- International Student Percentage

### Visualizations

- Universities by Country
- Research Impact by University
- Research Productivity by University
- Research Citations Analysis

---

## 2. Research Analytics Dashboard

### Purpose

Provides an analysis of university research performance.

### Main KPIs

- Research Impact Score
- Research Productivity Index
- Academic Reputation Score

### Visualizations

- Research Impact by University
- Research Productivity by University
- Research Citations Analysis

---

## 3. Student Analytics Dashboard

### Purpose

Provides analysis of student-related university metrics.

### Main KPIs

- Faculty-to-Student Ratio
- International Student Percentage

### Visualizations

- Top International Student Universities
- Faculty-to-Student Ratio Analysis
- Female-Male Student Analysis

---

## 4. Country Comparison Dashboard

### Purpose

Allows comparison of university performance across different countries.

### Main KPIs

- Global Ranking Score
- Academic Reputation Score
- Research Impact Score

### Visualizations

- Universities by Country
- Average Ranking Score by Country
- Research Impact by Country

---

## Dashboard Interaction

The dashboards can be explored using Tableau filters and interactive elements.

Users can:

- Select universities
- Compare countries
- Explore KPI values
- View individual university performance
- Hover over charts to view additional information
- Navigate between the four dashboards

---

## Dashboard Navigation

The four dashboards are:

1. University Overview
2. Research Analytics
3. Student Analytics
4. Country Comparison

The dashboards are designed to provide a connected view of higher education performance.

---

## Tableau Workbook

The final Tableau workbook contains the four completed dashboards and the supporting worksheets used for KPI analysis and visualization.
