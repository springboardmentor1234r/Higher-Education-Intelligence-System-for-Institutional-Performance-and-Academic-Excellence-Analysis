# EduVision DV – KPI Definitions

## Overview

The EduVision DV project uses six key performance indicators (KPIs) to analyze university performance, research strength, student characteristics, and academic reputation.

## 1. Global Ranking Score

The Global Ranking Score represents the overall ranking performance of a university.

**Field:** Global Ranking Score

**Purpose:** Used to compare the overall ranking performance of universities.

## 2. Research Impact Score

The Research Impact Score measures the research impact of a university.

**Field:** Research Impact Score

**Purpose:** Used to analyze and compare the research performance of universities.

## 3. Faculty-to-Student Ratio

The Faculty-to-Student Ratio represents the relationship between faculty members and students.

**Field:** Faculty to Student Ratio

**Purpose:** Used to understand faculty availability relative to the student population.

## 4. International Student Percentage

The International Student Percentage represents the proportion of international students in a university.

**Field:** International Student Percentage

**Purpose:** Used to analyze international student presence and student diversity.

## 5. Academic Reputation Score

The Academic Reputation Score represents the academic reputation of a university.

**Field:** Academic Reputation Score

**Purpose:** Used to compare the academic reputation of universities.

## 6. Research Productivity Index

The Research Productivity Index represents the research productivity of a university.

**Field:** Research Productivity Index

**Purpose:** Used to compare research productivity across universities.

## KPI Usage

The six KPIs were implemented and visualized using Tableau. They are used across the University Overview, Research Analytics, Student Analytics, and Country Comparison dashboards.

## Validation

The KPI values were checked during the testing phase to ensure that the calculated fields were available correctly in Tableau and displayed properly in the dashboards.
