# Data Sources & Attribution

## 1. Primary Datasets
1. **QS World University Rankings (2025):**
   - Author / Publisher: Quacquarelli Symonds
   - URL: https://www.topuniversities.com/world-university-rankings
   - Fields: Rank, University Name, Overall Score, Academic Reputation, Faculty/Student Ratio.

2. **Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings (2024):**
   - Author / Publisher: Times Higher Education
   - URL: https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings
   - Fields: Overall Score, Teaching, Research Environment, Research Quality, Industry Income, International Outlook.

3. **World University Rankings (WUR 2023):**
   - Author / Publisher: Center for World University Rankings
   - Fields: Citations, Publications, Alumni Employment, Total Students, Female:Male Ratio.

4. **The World Bank Education Statistics (EdStats):**
   - Author / Publisher: The World Bank Group
   - URL: https://databank.worldbank.org/source/education-statistics
   - Fields: Country Code, Indicator Name, Educational Expenditures, Tertiary Enrollment.
