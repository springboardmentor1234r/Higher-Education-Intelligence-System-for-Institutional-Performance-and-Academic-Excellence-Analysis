# Milestone 03: Dashboard Design & Implementation Guide

## 1. Overview
Milestone 03 establishes the visual analytics layer in Tableau. The final packaged workbook (`EduVision_DV.twbx`) contains **four production dashboards** configured in a modern Light Mode theme.

---

## 2. Dashboard Suite Specifications

### Dashboard 1: 🎓 University Overview
- **Purpose:** Macro-level institutional benchmarking, rankings, and global geographic distribution.
- **Top Scorecards (6 KPIs):** Global Ranking, Research Impact, Faculty Ratio, International Student %, Academic Reputation, Research Productivity.
- **Visual Visualizations:** Top Universities Bar Charts, Global University Distribution Map, Faculty-to-Student Ratio Analysis.

### Dashboard 2: 🔬 Research Analytics
- **Purpose:** Research performance evaluation, scholarly citations, and industry innovation.
- **Top Scorecards (6 KPIs):** Research Impact, Citations Score, Research Quality, Research Environment, Industry Impact, Research Productivity.
- **Visual Visualizations:** Research Impact by Country, Citations vs Productivity Correlation Scatter, Industry Impact vs Research Quality.

### Dashboard 3: 👩‍🎓 Student Analytics
- **Purpose:** Student demographics, enrollment volume, gender parity, and international diversity.
- **Top Scorecards (6 KPIs):**
  1. `Total Students`: Total FTE student population (~20.18M).
  2. `Avg Male Pct`: Mean male enrollment percentage (48.7%).
  3. `Avg Female Pct`: Mean female enrollment percentage (51.3%).
  4. `Avg Overall Score`: Composite academic score (44.9).
  5. `Total Intl Student Pct`: Mean international student ratio (14.1%).
  6. `Avg Intl Outlook`: Global international outlook benchmark (59.3).
- **Visual Visualizations:** International Student Percentage Bar Chart, Faculty-to-Student Ratio Distribution, Enrollment comparisons.

### Dashboard 4: 🌍 Country Comparison
- **Purpose:** Cross-national education benchmarking, regional rankings, and macro education indicators.
- **Top Scorecards (6 KPIs):** National aggregated scores across all core educational pillars.
- **Visual Visualizations:** Average Performance by Country, Regional Distribution Box Plots, National Higher Education Capacity.

---

## 3. UI Design Standards
- **Canvas Background:** `#F8FAFC` (Clean slate modern canvas)
- **Container Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white card containers with subtle `#CBD5E1` borders)
- **Primary Brand Color:** `#4F46E5` (Indigo accent)
- **Secondary Action Color:** `#0284C7` (Sky blue interactive highlight)
- **Typography:** Segoe UI Semibold for titles and KPI scorecards, Segoe UI for body and mark labels.
