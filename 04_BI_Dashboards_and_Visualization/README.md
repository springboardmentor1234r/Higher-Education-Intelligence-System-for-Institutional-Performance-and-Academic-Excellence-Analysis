# Module 04 – BI Dashboards and Visualization

## Overview
Module 04 organizes the Business Intelligence layer, hosting Tableau workbook files, calculated field definitions, dashboard layout guides, and interactive visualization specifications across four core institutional views.

## Core Analytics Dashboards
1. **University Overview**: Institutional ranking leaderboard, KPI summary cards, reputation radar trends, and multi-metric side-by-side comparison tables.
2. **Research Analytics**: Research productivity vs citation impact scatter plots, subject area breakdowns, and citation distribution maps.
3. **Student Analytics**: International student percentage maps, faculty capacity distribution histograms, and diversity benchmark scatter charts.
4. **Country Comparison**: Country-level higher education sector heatmaps, public education expenditure vs ranking performance scatter plots, and World Bank indicator trends.
