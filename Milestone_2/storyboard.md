# EduVision_DV Storyboard / Prototype

## Dashboard 1 — University Overview
Header + six KPI cards. Left: ranked universities. Center/right: overall score vs academic reputation. Filters: University, Country, Region, Year, Source.

## Dashboard 2 — Research Analytics
Research Score, Citation Score, Research Impact and Research Productivity Index. Research-vs-citation scatter and top research universities.

## Dashboard 3 — Student Analytics
Total Students, International Students, International Student %, Students per Staff. Population and international diversity comparisons.

## Dashboard 4 — Country Comparison
Country university count, average ranking/score, research performance and education-indicator trends.

## Navigation
Overview → Research → Student → Country. Add Home/Back navigation buttons. University selections should carry to Research and Student; country should carry to Country Comparison.
