# EduVision DV – Dataset Sources

## Project datasets

### 1. QS World University Rankings 2025
Used primarily for the University Overview dashboard.

Key fields include:
- University name
- Country
- 2025 ranking
- Academic reputation
- Employer reputation
- Faculty/student-related score
- Citations per faculty
- International faculty
- International students
- International research network
- Employment outcomes
- Sustainability
- Overall score

### 2. THE World University Rankings 2024
Used primarily for Research Analytics.

Key measures include:
- Overall score
- Teaching score
- Research score
- Citations score
- Industry income
- International outlook
- Total students
- Student-staff ratio
- International students percentage
- Female/male student percentages

### 3. World University Rankings 2023
Used primarily for Students Analytics and student-related university performance.

### 4. World Bank Education Statistics
Used for Country Comparison at country/year/indicator level.

Key indicators include:
- Education expenditure
- Tertiary enrollment
- Adult literacy
- Primary completion
- Tertiary teachers
- GDP per capita
- Population-related indicators

## Data-model principle

The project uses the datasets according to their intended analytical grain rather than forcing all sources into one large many-to-many table.
