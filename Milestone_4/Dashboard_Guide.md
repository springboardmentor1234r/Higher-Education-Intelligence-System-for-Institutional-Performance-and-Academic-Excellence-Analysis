# EduVision DV – Dashboard Guide

## Dashboard flow

**University Overview → Research Analytics → Students Analytics → Country Comparison**

Navigation buttons are available on each dashboard.

## University Overview

Purpose: high-level university performance analysis.

Use it to review:
- University rankings
- Country distribution
- Overall score
- Academic reputation
- Research impact
- Student/staff measures
- International student indicators

## Research Analytics

Purpose: research and academic performance analysis.

Use it to review:
- Research score
- Citations
- Teaching vs research
- International outlook
- Student/staff ratio
- Top research institutions

## Students Analytics

Purpose: student population and diversity analysis.

Use it to review:
- Total students
- International students
- Female students
- Student/staff ratio
- Student distribution
- Teaching/research/citation comparison

## Country Comparison

Purpose: country-level education benchmarking.

Use it to review:
- Education expenditure
- Tertiary enrollment
- Adult literacy
- Primary completion
- Tertiary teachers
- GDP per capita
- Country rankings/comparisons

## Interaction note

Visual-selection filter actions are configured in the workbook. Because some actions target the dashboard as a whole, KPI cards can become blank for certain selections. Review these actions before final portfolio release.
