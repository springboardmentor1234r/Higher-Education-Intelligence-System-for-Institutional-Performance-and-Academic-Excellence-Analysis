# EduVision DV – KPI Definitions

## Major project KPIs

### 1. Global Ranking Score
Represents the university ranking/performance measure defined for the project. In the University Overview workbook, the displayed Global Ranking worksheet uses `Rank 2025 Numeric`.

### 2. Research Impact Score
Uses `Citations Per Faculty Score` in the University Overview workbook.

### 3. Faculty-to-Student Ratio
Uses the actual `Student Staff Ratio` field where available rather than substituting an unrelated score.

### 4. International Student Percentage
Uses `International Students Percentage`, which represents a percentage rather than an international-student score.

### 5. Academic Reputation Score
Uses the QS `Academic Reputation` field.

### 6. Research Productivity Index
This KPI requires a documented, reproducible formula based on legitimate research variables. The project should not silently substitute an unrelated score for this KPI.

## Dashboard-specific KPI measures

Research Analytics:
- Research Score
- Citations Score
- Student–Staff Ratio
- International Students %

Students Analytics:
- Total Students
- Student Staff Ratio
- International Students Percentage
- Female Students %

Country Comparison:
- Education Expenditure % GDP
- Tertiary Enrollment
- Adult Literacy Rate
- Primary Completion Rate
- Tertiary Teachers
- GDP per Capita

## Formatting principle

Scores, ratios and percentages must retain their original meaning. A score should not be presented as a percentage unless the underlying field is actually a percentage.
