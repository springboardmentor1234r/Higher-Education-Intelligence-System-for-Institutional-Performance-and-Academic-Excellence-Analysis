# EduVision_DV KPI Definitions

## 1. Introduction

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are used in the EduVision_DV Tableau
dashboards to provide concise summaries of important university
performance measures.

The KPIs are distributed across the University Overview, Research
Analytics, and Student Analytics dashboards.

---

## 2. Global Ranking Score

### Description

The Global Ranking Score represents the university's overall ranking
performance based on the ranking-related score available in the prepared
dataset.

### Dashboard

University Overview

### Purpose

It provides a quick indication of the university's overall global
performance and supports comparison between universities.

---

## 3. Academic Reputation Score

### Description

Academic Reputation Score represents the academic reputation measure
available for a university in the prepared ranking data.

### Dashboard

University Overview and Student Analytics

### Purpose

It helps users understand how a university is positioned in terms of
academic reputation.

---

## 4. Research Impact Score

### Description

Research Impact Score represents the research impact measure used in the
EduVision_DV analysis.

### Dashboard

University Overview and Research Analytics

### Purpose

It provides an indication of the influence and impact of a university's
research performance.

---

## 5. Research Productivity Index

### Description

Research Productivity Index represents the research productivity measure
prepared for the EduVision_DV analysis.

### Dashboard

University Overview and Research Analytics

### Purpose

It helps users evaluate and compare research productivity across
universities.

---

## 6. Faculty-Student Ratio

### Description

Faculty-Student Ratio represents the relationship between the number of
faculty members and students associated with a university.

### Dashboard

University Overview and Student Analytics

### Purpose

It provides an indication of faculty availability relative to the student
population and supports comparison of student-to-faculty conditions.

---

## 7. International Student Percentage

### Description

International Student Percentage represents the proportion of students
who are international students within the university data.

### Dashboard

University Overview and Student Analytics

### Purpose

It provides an indication of the international composition of a
university's student population.

---

## 8. KPI Interpretation

KPIs should be interpreted together with the selected filters and
dashboard context.

For example:

- Selecting a specific university changes the displayed KPI values
  according to the available data for that university.
- Selecting a country allows users to examine universities associated
  with that country.
- Selecting a year changes the analysis to the corresponding period where
  data is available.

---

## 9. KPI Usage in Dashboard Analysis

The KPIs provide quick summaries, while the accompanying charts provide
more detailed comparisons and trends.

The combination of KPIs, charts, and filters allows users to examine:

- Overall university performance
- Academic reputation
- Research impact
- Research productivity
- Faculty-student conditions
- International student representation

---

## 10. Note on KPI Calculations

The KPI values displayed in Tableau are based on the prepared and
validated EduVision_DV dataset and the calculations implemented in the
final Tableau workbook.

The KPI definitions describe the analytical meaning of each measure.
The exact calculation logic should be interpreted together with the
underlying dataset fields and Tableau calculated fields used in the
workbook.
