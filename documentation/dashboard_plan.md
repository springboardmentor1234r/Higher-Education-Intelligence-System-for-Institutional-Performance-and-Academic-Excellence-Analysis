# Tableau Dashboard Plan

### 1. University Overview
KPI cards: ranking, overall score, academic reputation, research impact, students/staff, international students.
Filters: University, Country, Region, Year, Source.

### 2. Research Analytics
Research score, citation score, research impact, productivity index, top research universities.
Filters: University, Country, Year, Source.

### 3. Student Analytics
Total students, international students, international %, students/staff, diversity comparisons.
Filters: University, Country, Year, Source.

### 4. Country Comparison
University count, ranking/score comparison, research performance, international indicators, education trends.
Filters: Country, Region, Year, Indicator.

Use dashboard actions for University → Research/Student and Country → Country Comparison.
