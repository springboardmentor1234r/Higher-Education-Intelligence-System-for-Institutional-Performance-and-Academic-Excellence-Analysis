# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Formula / meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Global Ranking Score | Publisher overall score | 0–100 |
| Research Impact Score | Citation-related source score | 0–100 |
| Faculty-to-Student Ratio | Students per staff, where available | students/staff |
| International Student Percentage | Reported international student percentage | % |
| Academic Reputation Score | QS Academic Reputation Score | 0–100 |
| Research Productivity Index | 50% research + 30% citations + 20% research/international collaboration indicator, after source/year min-max normalization | 0–100 |

The derived productivity index is an analytical KPI, not an official publisher metric. Missing components cause weight renormalization.
