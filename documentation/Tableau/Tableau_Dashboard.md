## Dashboard Description

The EduVision interactive Tableau workbook provides multi-dimensional analytical perspectives on higher education institutional performance, research output, campus internationalization, and national education indicators.

### Key Visual Perspectives
- **University Overview**: Overall institutional ranking, global score distributions, and peer comparison.
- **Research Analytics**: Citations per faculty, research volume, and international research collaboration impact.
- **Student Analytics**: Enrollment composition, faculty-to-student ratios, and campus diversity metrics.
- **Country Comparison**: Macroeconomic education expenditure vs national institutional performance using World Bank EdStats indicators.
