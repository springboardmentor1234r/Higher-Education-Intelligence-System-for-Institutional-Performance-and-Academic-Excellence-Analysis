# EduVision – KPI Definitions

## Project

Higher Education Intelligence System for Institutional Performance and Academic Excellence Analysis

---

## 1. Overview

EduVision uses six Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to evaluate university ranking performance, research impact, student characteristics, and institutional research productivity.

The KPIs were generated using Python and validated in Tableau.

---

## 2. Global Ranking Score

### Definition

The Global Ranking Score represents the overall QS 2025 performance score of a university.

### Source

QS World University Rankings 2025.
