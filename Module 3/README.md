## 1. Description of Work
In this module, I completed both **Milestone 3 / Module 5 (University Overview & Research Analytics)** and **Milestone 3 / Module 6 (Student Analytics, Country Comparison & Full Suite Integration)**:
- Developed the complete 4-dashboard suite in Tableau Desktop:
  1. **University Overview:** Executive scorecard, Top 10 rankings bar chart, Global university map, Academic reputation vs overall score scatter, and institutional table.
  2. **Research Analytics:** Research productivity ranking, Citations vs research environment scatter, and regional collaboration impact.
  3. **Student Analytics:** International student % distribution, students per staff ratios, and continental diversity breakdown.
  4. **Country Comparison:** National university capacity, government education expenditure (% of GDP) vs performance, and gross tertiary enrollment trends (2010–2023).
- Configured dashboard navigation buttons enabling one-click switching between views.
- Implemented filter actions passing `university_id` and `country_id` across views.
